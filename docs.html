Reqo documentation

Reqo is a memecoin launchpad on QoreChain testnet (Diana). You launch a token by signing a request in your wallet, and every launch starts with a pool that holds the full supply and a fixed WQOR seed. Trading is done with signed swap requests, so you never need an EVM window or gas to trade.

How to launch a token

  1. Open the app and connect a wallet on QoreChain Diana (chain ID 9800). The app asks the wallet to switch if needed.
  2. Fill in the name, ticker, supply (whole tokens), description, and an optional image.
  3. Sign the request in your wallet. Signing is free and sends no transaction.
  4. If the relayer window is open, the token is created at once. If it's closed, your launch is queued and created when the window opens. The Launch button stays dimmed while it waits.

Tickers are shown with a $ prefix, for example $PFK. The on-chain symbol is stored without the prefix.

How to trade

Trades use WQOR, a wrapped form of QOR where each unit is backed by QOR held in the contract.

  1. Request a WQOR allowance from the app (up to 5 WQOR per address, in total).
  2. Sign a permit and a swap request for the amount you want to trade. The swap sets a minimum amount you will receive, and it fails if the price moves past that minimum.
  3. The relayer submits the swap. You receive tokens at or above your minimum.

The trade screen is coming soon. Until then, trades can be made through the contracts described below.

Fees and safety

  • Each swap pays a 0.3% fee. The fee stays in the pool and cannot be withdrawn.
  • The pool's liquidity cannot be withdrawn by anyone, including the token creator.
  • Creators receive no tokens at launch. The full supply is placed in the pool.
  • There is no mint after launch, no owner, and no upgrade path on the token or pool.
  • Each launch seeds the pool with 10 WQOR, paid by the account that submits the launch.
  • The contracts have not been audited. Testnet only.
